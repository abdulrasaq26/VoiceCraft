    Fix unusual activity
    🧹

    Google Flow sometimes shows an "unusual activity" block when you generate a lot in a row. It's a Google-side limit — not the extension. Clearing Flow's site data usually clears it.

    Steps
    1. Click Open Clear Data Page below.
    2. Set Time range → Last hour (or 24 hours).
    3. Tick Cookies and Cached images and files.
    4. Click Delete data.
    5. Sign in to Flow again and press Start.

    If it persists, wait a few minutes and try again — only Google can fully clear it.
