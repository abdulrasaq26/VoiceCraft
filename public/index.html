🎭 Delivery styles

Click to insert a marker at the cursor. Everything after it is voiced by that variant of the same speaker, until the next marker.

🎛️ Engine parameters

temperature — low is flat and consistent, high is varied but riskier.

top_p — narrower keeps delivery safer.

repetition_penalty — raise if the voice loops or stutters.

Fixed seed reproduces the same take; blank re-rolls each run.

Advanced

chunk_length — longer gives more context, costs more VRAM.

max_new_tokens — guards against runaway output.
